About DocuReady

A document linter, not an eligibility authority.

Official portals make the final decisions. DocuReady is an applicant-side preparation aid that checks consistency across the documents you provide, shows where evidence came from, and explains what may need attention.

01 / CONSISTENCY

Compare facts across documents

Surface name, date, amount and reference mismatches across a multi-document packet using explicit, testable rules.

02 / EVIDENCE

Make each finding inspectable

Review extracted values with page and bounding-box provenance, alongside confidence and uncertainty signals.

03 / NEXT STEPS

Turn findings into actions

Understand which document needs attention and what practical correction or human review may be appropriate.

The workflow

Four clear steps. No black-box verdicts.

The local application combines document extraction with deterministic comparison and a safety layer that can abstain when the evidence is not strong enough.

1

Add a document packet

Select a synthetic example or provide PDFs in the local application.

2

Extract and inspect

Read vector text or use local OCR, with evidence provenance retained.

3

Check and review

Run defined rules; ambiguous or weak evidence is sent for review.

4

Correct and re-check

Replace a document and re-run the packet audit locally.

Privacy is an architecture choice

Your documents should stay under your control.

The authoritative workflow is local execution: the Python/Streamlit application processes documents on the machine where it is running. This public website is only an informational page.

✓No document upload on this websiteThis Vercel site has no upload form, API or document-processing endpoint.
✓Local processing for the core workflowRun the audit locally so files are handled by your own machine.
✓Evidence-led, cautious outcomesUncertain evidence is surfaced for review instead of being presented as certainty.
ⓘ
Important: DocuReady does not decide eligibility.

It is not a government service, legal authority, identity-authentication service or guarantee of approval. A “no issues found” result means only that the configured checks passed on the supplied evidence. Always follow the relevant official scheme and portal instructions.
