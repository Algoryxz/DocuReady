# Statutory and Technical Research References

## Statutory & Regulatory Citations

1. **National Scholarship Portal (NSP) Standard Operating Procedure**:
   - *Authority*: Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) & Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE), Government of India.
   - *Applicable Clauses*: Clause 3.1 (Family Annual Income Ceiling of ₹2.5 Lakhs for Centrally Sponsored Post-Matric Scholarships); Clause 4.2 (Identity and Name Harmonization Guidelines across Aadhaar and Educational Credentials).

2. **Guidelines for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) in Scholarship Schemes**:
   - *Authority*: Cabinet Secretariat, Government of India (DBT Mission).
   - *Applicable Order*: Section 6 (Bank Account Validation & Mandate of Account Holder Matching Applicant Credential to prevent diversion of funds).

3. **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act), 2023**:
   - *Act No.*: 22 of 2023, Parliament of India.
   - *Provisions*: Section 4 (Grounds for Processing Personal Data); Section 8 (Obligations of Data Fiduciary regarding accuracy, purpose limitation, and reasonable security safeguards).

4. **Secondary Education Examination Board Guidelines for Age Verification**:
   - *Authority*: Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) / State Examination Boards.
   - *Rule*: Rule 8 (Matriculation / 10th Class Passing Certificate as the conclusive legal proof of Date of Birth in civil applications).

5. **State Revenue Department Manuals on Revenue Certificates**:
   - *Standard*: Model e-District Certificate Guidelines.
   - *Rule*: Income Certificates valid strictly for the financial year of issue (12-month validity window from date of Tahsildar / Revenue Inspector signature).

## Academic & Technical Literature

1. **Platt, John C. (1999)**:
   - *"Probabilistic Outputs for Support Vector Machines and Comparisons to Regularized Likelihood Methods."* Advances in Large Margin Classifiers.
   - *DocuReady Application*: Logistic calibration mapping multi-dimensional OCR heuristic features to true empirical correctness probabilities.

2. **Guo, Chuan, et al. (2017)**:
   - *"On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks."* ICML 2017.
   - *DocuReady Application*: Formulation of Expected Calibration Error (ECE) and temperature scaling principles for document extraction confidence.

3. **Smith, Ray (2007)**:
   - *"An Overview of the Tesseract OCR Engine."* Ninth International Conference on Document Analysis and Recognition (ICDAR 2007).
   - *DocuReady Application*: Native layout analysis, character bounding box mapping, and LSTM token stream extraction in degraded scan conditions.

4. **Otsu, Nobuyuki (1979)**:
   - *"A Threshold Selection Method from Gray-Level Histograms."* IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics.
   - *DocuReady Application*: Adaptive local binarization of unevenly lit and photostat applicant documents.
