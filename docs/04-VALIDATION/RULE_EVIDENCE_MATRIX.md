## 2. Statutory Audit Flags & Non-Claims

1. **No Bank Balance or Live NPCI Mandate Claims**: DocuReady inspects format and passbook consistency. It does NOT claim live Core Banking System (CBS) integration, NPCI Aadhaar mapper lookup, or active lien verification.
2. **No Aadhaar Biometric Claims**: Aadhaar checks are limited to mathematical Verhoeff checksum (ISO/IEC 7064) and cross-document demographic text comparison. DocuReady does not connect to UIDAI CIDR or claim biometric authentication.
3. **Jurisdiction-Specific Validity**: In Odisha, income certificates are valid for 3 years (Rule 8(2), Odisha Misc Cert Rules 2019). In states where annual re-issuance is mandatory, the scheme configuration must be set accordingly (`income_cert_validity_years: 1`).
4. **Principle of Non-Rejection Under Uncertainty**: In any scenario where evidence is ambiguous, missing, or governing rules depend on unverified local relaxations, DocuReady outputs `REVIEW` or `UNKNOWN`, never automatic rejection.
